Nitterium lets you browse public Twitter/X content through Nitter and Teapot instances without a Twitter/X account. Save profiles as local subscriptions, organize them into groups, and browse their posts in combined feeds.

Important: Nitterium depends on independently operated Nitter and Teapot instances. Availability, supported features, and feed limits vary between instances. If one stops working or becomes rate limited, try another.

Features:

* Privacy First: No ads, tracking, or account required.
* Nitter and Teapot Integration: Browse via your preferred instance.
* Local subscriptions: Save and re-arrange accounts locally via drag-and-drop.
* Grouped feeds: Browse posts from a selected group. Give groups their own names, icons, and colors.
* Long-Press & Sharing: Easily share, copy, or open links and media directly via long-press actions.
* Image Viewer: Swipe to dismiss and direct downloads.
* Pull-to-Refresh: Swipe down to reload content seamlessly.
* Dynamic Theming: Material 3 design adapts to system theme.
* Backup and restore: Export subscriptions, groups, and settings, and review a backup before restoring it.
* Supported links: Open supported Twitter/X and Nitter links in the app.
* RTL Support: Supports RTL content by default.
* Instance selection: Choose a built-in instance or add your own.
